Trusti privacy policy

This English translation is provided for information only. Only the French version is legally binding.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Trusti is an app and website that assesses how reliable other apps and software are and how well they respect privacy, and helps you find more respectful alternatives.

This policy explains what data the app processes, why it is processed, who it may be shared with and how users can exercise their rights.

In short

Trusti requires no account and no login. The app sends no data about your selection of apps or your preferences to its servers. This information stays stored locally on your device.

Trusti does no ad tracking, sells no personal data and does not use your selections for profiling.

Some technical data may nevertheless be processed by the providers that host and run the service, in particular to ensure its security and availability.

Data controller

The controller of the personal data processed in connection with the Trusti app is: Trusti'CLE, publisher of the Trusti app.

For any question about the protection of your personal data or to exercise your rights, you can contact them at the following address (we reply in French or English): contact@trusti-score.eu

What data is processed?

No user account

Trusti asks for no email address, no password and no personal identifier to access the app.

The app queries a public catalogue of assessments in order to display the grades and information about the apps listed.

Data stored locally

Your selection of apps and the notes or preferences you save are kept only on your device, in your browser's local storage (localStorage).

This mechanism makes it possible, in particular, to remember your choices and find them again the next times you use Trusti.

This information is not sent to Trusti's servers and cannot be accessed remotely by the app's publisher.

This storage is used solely to make the app's features work. It is used neither for advertising nor to track your browsing.

Technical connection data

Like any web service, Trusti uses technical providers that may record certain information needed for hosting, security and the proper running of the service.

This information may include, in particular:

  • The IP address;
  • The date and time of connections or requests;
  • The URLs requested;
  • Certain technical information about the browser, the device or the requests made.

This data may be processed by our hosting providers, under the conditions described in the section "Hosting and technical providers".

Trusti does not use this information for profiling or ad tracking.

Why is this data processed?

The processing carried out serves only the following purposes:

  • Displaying the public catalogue of assessments and running the app;
  • Remembering the selected apps and the user's preferences locally;
  • Ensuring the security, availability and proper technical running of the service.

Local storage is used to provide the features requested by the user.

Technical processing related to hosting and to the security of the service is based on the publisher's legitimate interest in ensuring the security and availability of its app.

No data is used for advertising, profiling or resale to third parties.

Who is the data shared with?

Trusti neither sells nor shares any personal data about users' preferences or selections.

Data saved in the browser's local storage is not sent to Trusti's servers.

Only the technical providers needed to host and run the service may process certain technical connection data, as part of their services.

Local storage and cookies

Trusti uses the browser's local storage mechanism (localStorage) to keep certain information needed for the app to work, in particular the selected apps and the user's preferences.

Unlike traditional cookies, this information is saved directly in the browser and is not automatically sent to the server with every request.

This storage is purely functional and allows no advertising or behavioural tracking.

It is used so that users can find their selections and preferences again the next times they use Trusti.

You can delete this information at any time by clearing your browsing data or the local storage associated with Trusti in your browser settings. Deleting it may mean losing your saved selections and preferences.

Trusti does not use advertising cookies or trackers designed to follow users' browsing.

Hosting and technical providers

To run Trusti, we use a specialised technical provider and, temporarily, the technical relay described at the end of this section.

Hyperfluid – Hosting, technical infrastructure and database hosting

Trusti relies on the Hyperfluid platform, published by the French company Nudibranches Technologies, to run and host its technical infrastructure.

As part of this service, the platform may process certain technical data needed for the running, security and availability of the service, in particular IP addresses, the dates and times of connections and requests, and certain technical information about exchanges with the app.

Hyperfluid also hosts and manages the PostgreSQL database containing the public catalogue of assessments of the apps listed on Trusti.

This database contains information about the apps assessed and their grades. It does not contain users' personal selections or preferences, which stay stored locally on their device.

The data is hosted on infrastructure located in Europe, under Hyperfluid's shared offering. This infrastructure is operated by the Nudibranches Technologies teams.

The hosting conditions, the terms of access to the data and any use of technical subcontractors are governed by the provider's contractual commitments.

To find out more:

  • Hyperfluid official website
  • Hyperfluid's sovereignty dossier (in French)
  • Nudibranches Technologies privacy policy

Vercel – Temporary relay for older versions of the Android app

Versions of the Android app released before 13 November 2026 send their requests to a relay hosted by Vercel Inc. (United States), which forwards them to the Hyperfluid infrastructure. This relay processes only technical connection data (IP address, date and time of requests, URLs requested); it stores neither the catalogue nor your selections.

This data may be processed outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the United States. These transfers are governed by the mechanisms provided for by the applicable regulations, in particular standard contractual clauses or the applicable certification mechanisms.

This relay is temporary: it will be removed once these older versions are no longer in use. Updating the app is enough to stop using it.

To find out more: Vercel privacy policy

These providers act as part of their hosting and technical operation of the service, in accordance with the applicable contractual commitments.

Retention period

Information saved in the browser's local storage is kept on the user's device until the user deletes it or until it is replaced while using the app.

Any technical connection data recorded by the hosting providers is kept according to their own policies and the technical settings applicable to the service, within the limits of the periods needed for its security and operational purposes.

Any former account data, in particular email addresses collected before the login feature was removed, is kept only for as long as needed to delete it or to meet any legal obligations.

Your rights

As Trusti has no user account system, no personal data linked to a profile or a registration is currently collected during normal use of the app.

Information saved locally on your device can be deleted directly from your browser's storage settings.

Any technical data processed by the hosting providers remains subject to the rights provided for by the applicable personal data protection regulations.

Under the conditions set out in the GDPR, you have in particular the following rights:

  • Right of access to your personal data;
  • Right to rectification;
  • Right to erasure;
  • Right to restriction of processing;
  • Right to object, where applicable.

To exercise your rights or ask a question about the processing of your personal data, you can contact us at the address given in the section "Contact us".

If you had logged in with your email address before this feature was removed and would like it to be erased from our database, you can contact us. We will delete it within 30 days, subject to any legal retention obligations.

You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the Commission nationale de l'informatique et des libertés (CNIL): www.cnil.fr.

Children

Trusti does not specifically target children and does not knowingly collect any personal data from children.
